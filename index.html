DRIFT — Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 24, 2026

Overview

DRIFT ("the App") is a single-player mobile arcade game. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data is collected, how it is used, and your rights.

Information We Collect

DRIFT does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. The App operates entirely on your device.

The following data is stored locally on your device only:

  • Your best score
  • Your coin balance
  • Purchased orb skins and background themes
  • Sound mute preference

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

No Account Required

DRIFT does not require you to create an account, provide an email address, or sign in with any service.

Third-Party Services

DRIFT does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising networks, crash reporting services, or tracking SDKs.

Children's Privacy

DRIFT does not knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13. Because no personal data is collected from any user, the App is safe for all ages.

Data Deletion

All locally stored data (scores, coins, purchases) can be deleted at any time by uninstalling the App from your device.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the revised version will be made available with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at the email address listed on the App Store product page.
